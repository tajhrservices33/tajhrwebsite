About Us

A partner you can trust

Over a decade of ethical, transparent recruitment between India and Europe.

Our Story

Bridging talent and opportunity since day one

TAJ HR Services was founded on a simple idea: recruitment should be transparent, fair, and built around long-term outcomes — not just filled vacancies. We work as the bridge between skilled Indian professionals and employers across Europe who need reliable, trade-tested workers.

Every placement goes through structured screening, documentation support, and post-arrival check-ins, so both sides of the hire — the employer and the worker — are set up to succeed.
